# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be honest, be useful, be kind. Participation in this project means upholding
that standard in issues, pull requests, and every other space maintained here.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith; correct poor assumptions with evidence.
- Keep discussions on topic and accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or hate speech.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report violations to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports are handled
privately. Repeated or severe violations result in removal from project
spaces.

This text adapts the spirit of the Contributor Covenant; it applies to this
repository and any project space maintained by the owner.
